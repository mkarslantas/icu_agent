# Sepsis Management Protocol

Based on **Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 Guidelines**

## Definition
**Sepsis:** Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by dysregulated host response to infection
- **Criteria:** Suspected infection + SOFA score increase ≥2 points

**Septic Shock:** Sepsis with persisting hypotension requiring vasopressors (MAP ≥65 mmHg) AND lactate >2 mmol/L despite adequate fluid resuscitation

## Initial Resuscitation (Hour 1 Bundle)

### 1. Measure Lactate
- Initial lactate measurement
- Remeasure if elevated (>2 mmol/L) within 2-4 hours

### 2. Obtain Blood Cultures BEFORE Antibiotics
- At least 2 sets (aerobic and anaerobic)
- Additional cultures from suspected sources

### 3. Administer Broad-Spectrum Antibiotics
- Within **1 hour** of recognition
- Empiric therapy based on suspected source and local resistance patterns
- Reassess daily for de-escalation

### 4. Fluid Resuscitation
- **30 mL/kg crystalloid** for hypotension or lactate ≥4 mmol/L
- Within first 3 hours
- Crystalloids preferred over colloids
- Balanced crystalloids vs 0.9% saline - either acceptable

### 5. Vasopressors if Hypotensive During or After Resuscitation
- **Target MAP ≥65 mmHg**
- Norepinephrine: first-line vasopressor
- Can add vasopressin or epinephrine if inadequate response

## Subsequent Management

### Hemodynamic Support
- **Norepinephrine:** First-line (↑ MAP, ↓ HR vs dopamine)
- **Vasopressin:** Add if high-dose norepinephrine (0.03 units/min)
- **Epinephrine:** If additional agent needed
- **Dobutamine:** If cardiac dysfunction with elevated filling pressures and low cardiac output

### Steroid Therapy
- **Hydrocortisone 200 mg/day** (IV continuous or divided doses)
- If inadequate response to fluids and vasopressors
- Conditional recommendation

### Source Control
- Identify and control source within 12 hours if possible
- Drainage, debridement, device removal as indicated

### Antibiotic Stewardship
- De-escalate based on culture results and clinical improvement
- **Duration:** Typically 7-10 days
- Shorter courses (5-7 days) if rapid clinical resolution
- Use procalcitonin to guide duration in selected patients

### Supportive Care
- **Mechanical Ventilation:** Low tidal volume (6 mL/kg PBW) if ARDS
- **Sedation:** Light sedation targets, daily awakening trials
- **DVT Prophylaxis:** Pharmacologic prophylaxis
- **Stress Ulcer Prophylaxis:** PPI or H2 blocker if risk factors
- **Glycemic Control:** Target <180 mg/dL
- **Nutrition:** Enteral nutrition within 24-48 hours if tolerated

## Key Evidence
- **PROCESS, ARISE, ProMISe trials:** Early goal-directed therapy components (CVP, ScvO2) not beneficial
- **VANISH trial:** Vasopressin vs norepinephrine - similar outcomes
- **ADRENAL trial:** Hydrocortisone in septic shock - faster shock resolution, no mortality benefit
